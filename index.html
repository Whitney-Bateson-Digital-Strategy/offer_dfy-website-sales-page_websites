Questions? Let’s cover them.

What if I don’t have a logo or branding?
No problem. As part of onboarding, you’ll choose from our curated color palettes and font pairings, and we’ll create a clean text-based logo using your business name. If you want something more custom, there’s a Brand Starter add-on for that.
Do I need to provide my own photos?
If you have headshots and brand photos you love, send them over. If not, we source professional stock photos that fit your niche, location, and brand. We’ll let you approve the direction before we use them.
What platform is the site built on?
Your site is built on a modern, custom-coded framework — not WordPress, not Squarespace, not a drag-and-drop builder. That means it’s faster, more secure, and easier to maintain. You don’t need to know anything about the tech — that’s our job.
Do I need to be on a maintenance plan?
Yes — all sites include a monthly hosting and maintenance subscription. This covers your hosting, security updates, and a set number of content updates each month. It’s what keeps your site running smoothly without you ever having to think about it.
What counts as a content update?
Things like changing a service description, swapping a photo, updating your hours or pricing, or adding a new testimonial. Structural changes (adding a new page, redesigning a section) are handled separately.
What if I want to make changes after launch?
Small updates are included in your monthly plan. For bigger changes, just ask — we’ll let you know if it falls inside or outside your plan.
Do we need to meet on a call before you start?
Most of the process is handled through our questionnaire, which is designed to get us everything we need without scheduling back-and-forth. An optional 30-minute strategy call is available if you’d like to talk through anything before we start.
How is this different from a custom website design?
Custom design means your designer builds something from scratch — typically 2–4 months and $5,000–$10,000+. Our process uses a proven template framework that’s fully customized to your brand, copy, and photos. You get a polished, professional result at a fraction of the time and cost.
What if I’m not ready to commit yet?
Book the free call anyway. There’s no obligation — it’s a 20-minute conversation to figure out if this is the right fit and the right timing for you.
