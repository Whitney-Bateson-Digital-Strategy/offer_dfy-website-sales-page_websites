The Process

Here's how it works

We designed this to be as low-lift as possible on your end. Here's what the three weeks look like.

1
Week 1
You tell us about your business

You'll fill out our onboarding questionnaire and watch a few short guided videos that walk you through exactly what we need — your services, your clients, your story, and your goals. No marketing degree required. Just tell us what you do and who you help.

If you already have branding (logo, colors, fonts), send it over. If you don't, no problem — we'll have you choose from our curated options and build something cohesive from there. This is the only part where we need your attention. After this, you can go back to seeing clients.

2
Week 2
We write, design, and build

We take everything you gave us and get to work: writing your copy, sourcing photos, customizing the design, and building out your pages. You won't hear much from us this week — that's intentional.

3
Week 3
You review, we refine, and it goes live

We send you a preview of your site. You tell us what to adjust. We make the changes and launch. Done.
